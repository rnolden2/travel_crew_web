Delete your Travel Crew account

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Travel Crew account and the personal data associated with it. Travel Crew is published by Kai Technologies Corp. and is available on Google Play and the App Store. You can delete your account yourself inside the app, or ask us to delete it for you by email. Both options are free and permanent.

Option 1: Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Travel Crew app and sign in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the list and tap Delete Account.
  4. Tap Delete to confirm. This cannot be undone.

Your account is deactivated immediately and you are signed out on the device. You will not be able to sign back in with the same account.

Option 2: Ask us to delete your account

If you no longer have the app installed, or cannot sign in, email us and we will delete the account for you.

Email a deletion request

Send the request to Support@kaitechcorp.com with the subject line “Delete my Travel Crew account”. Include the email address or phone number you use to sign in and whether you sign in with Email, Google or Apple. To protect your account we may ask you to confirm the request from that same email address or from inside the app before we proceed. Verified requests are completed within 30 days, and we will confirm by email when the deletion is done.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted:

  • Your account is deactivated and sign-in is disabled for it.
  • Your public profile, including your name, photo, hometown, bio and social links, is no longer shown to other users.
  • You are removed from trip chats and other members can no longer message you.
  • Your favourites, follows, blocks, join requests, recommendation preferences, push-notification tokens and in-app notifications are removed.
  • Shared trip links for trips you own stop working and those trips are removed from Explore.

What may be kept

  • Content you added to trips owned by other crew members, such as activities, expenses and messages already sent, may remain visible to those members so their shared plans stay accurate. Your name is no longer linked to a live profile.
  • Limited records may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution or backups, as described in our Privacy Policy. Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis.
  • Copies of content that other users already downloaded, screenshotted or shared outside Travel Crew are not under our control.

Deleting data without deleting your account

You can remove individual trips, activities, expenses, photos and profile details from inside the app at any time. You can also email Support@kaitechcorp.com to ask for specific personal data to be corrected or removed while keeping your account.

Questions

Contact Support@kaitechcorp.com. See also our Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.
