YOUR AI PLAN, READY FOR YOUR CREW

Turn a chat into a trip.

Plan with your preferred AI assistant, then bring the itinerary into Travel Crew. Review the imported details before saving, and keep editing with your crew.

Imports support trip dates, flights, lodging, activities, and expenses. Review AI-generated information before relying on it.

  1. Open Import from AIIn My Trips, tap the sparkle icon and copy the Travel Crew prompt.
  2. Plan with your assistantDescribe your trip, then ask for the itinerary in the prompt’s format.
  3. Paste, review, and createPaste the response into Travel Crew, review the filled-in details, and create your trip.

CONNECTED ASSISTANTS

Ask your assistant to save the trip.

Connect a compatible AI assistant to Travel Crew, sign in, and allow trip creation. Ask it to save an itinerary: your trip arrives privately in My Trips. You can disconnect the assistant at any time.

YOU CHOOSE THE AUDIENCE

Share a trip, keep the crew in control.

A private trip stays out of Explore. Creating a share link makes its shared itinerary visible to people with the link, even when the trip is private. Expenses and chat stay within the crew. Joining still requires approval from the trip owner.
